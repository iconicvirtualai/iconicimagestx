Cancellation & refund policy

All ’Tis the Season packages are paid in advance to hold your date. Here's how changes work.

Individual sessions (Package A)

  • 48+ hours before your session: full refund, or a free reschedule within Nov 27 – Dec 14.
  • Under 48 hours: one free reschedule, subject to availability; otherwise the payment becomes studio credit good through Mar 31, 2027.
  • No-shows are non-refundable.
  • Swap the client family on your session at no charge, up to 24 hours before.

Group days & hosted events (Packages B, C & parties)

  • 14+ days before the event: full refund, minus any decor or menu costs already incurred.
  • 7–13 days before: 50% refund, or 100% studio credit good through Mar 31, 2027.
  • Under 7 days: non-refundable. Chef menus and custom decor are ordered at the 7-day mark.
  • Headcount can go down by up to 2 families without penalty, up to 7 days before.
For everyone: If Iconic must cancel (weather, illness or emergency), you'll get a full refund or your choice of a new date. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7–10 business days. Processing fees are non-refundable. Free testimonial videos are tied to completed client shoots and have no cash value. To cancel or reschedule, call 281.356.0965 or email photos@iconicimagestx.com.
